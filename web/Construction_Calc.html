Construction Calculator Help

This calculator is designed for construction, carpentry, millwork, finish work, and other trades that often use imperial measurements written as decimals or mixed fractions.

What you can type

  • Whole numbers: 12
  • Decimals: 3.14, 0.625
  • Simple fractions: 1/2, 7/16
  • Mixed numbers: 1 1/2, 12 3/8
  • Math operators: +, -, *, /
  • Parentheses: ( and )
  • The multiplication symbol × is also accepted and automatically treated as *
  • The division symbol ÷ is also accepted and automatically treated as /

Examples

((1 + 3.14) * 1 1/2) / 10
12 3/8 + 7/16
96 / 3
(8 1/2 * 4) + 1 3/4
10 - 3 5/8
2 × (6 3/4 + 1/8)

How mixed fractions work

A value like 1 1/2 is interpreted as one and one-half. A value like 12 3/8 is interpreted as twelve and three-eighths.

Fractional precision

The precision control determines the denominator used when formatting the result as a fraction. For example:

  • 2 formats to the nearest 1/2
  • 4 formats to the nearest 1/4
  • 8 formats to the nearest 1/8
  • 16 formats to the nearest 1/16
  • 32 formats to the nearest 1/32
  • 64 formats to the nearest 1/64
  • 128 formats to the nearest 1/128

The calculator allows any integer from 2 to 128. For standard trade use, powers of two are usually the most practical.

Notes

  • Results are shown as a decimal and as a mixed fraction.
  • Fractions are automatically simplified.
  • If the input is invalid, the calculator will display an error message instead of a result.
  • This calculator evaluates math expressions only. It does not support units such as ft, in, or mm.
