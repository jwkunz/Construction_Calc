# Privacy Policy for Construction Calc

**Effective date:** November 24, 2026

Construction Calc is a calculator app. This Privacy Policy explains what data may be processed when you use the app.

## Information We Collect

Construction Calc does not require you to create an account, and we do not collect or store your calculator entries on our own servers.

The app contains no advertising, analytics, or in-app purchases, and does not use third-party data-collection services. All calculations run on your device, and the app works offline.

## How the App Uses Data

Calculator entries and settings stay on your device. Nothing is sent to us or to any third party.

## Children's Privacy

Construction Calc is not directed to children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact the repository owner through [github.com/jwkunz](https://github.com/jwkunz).
